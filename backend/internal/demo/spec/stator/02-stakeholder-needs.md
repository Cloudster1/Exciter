# Stakeholder needs

### SN-1 Publish a page when it is ready
type: Stakeholder need
priority: must

An author needs to work on a page without readers seeing it until it is ready, and to bring back an earlier version when a change turns out wrong.

### SN-2 Keep knowledge in an order people can navigate
type: Stakeholder need
priority: must

A team needs to keep its pages in spaces and a tree, to move and restore them, and to start a new space or page without rebuilding what the team always writes.

### SN-3 Write together
type: Stakeholder need
priority: must

Several people need to write one page at the same time without overwriting each other, and to start from a known shape instead of a blank page.

### SN-4 Discuss a page and hear of changes
type: Stakeholder need
priority: should

Readers need to comment on a page or a passage of it, and the people who care about a page need to be told when it changes or when they are asked.

### SN-5 Find what was written
type: Stakeholder need
priority: must

A reader needs to find a page by its words, its title or its label, and never to be shown a page they may not read.

### SN-6 Decide who may read and write
type: Stakeholder need
priority: must

An administrator needs to give people, groups and outsiders exactly the access they need to a space or a single page, to check what a person may do, and to open pages to the public on purpose.

### SN-7 Keep pages, issues and requirements connected
type: Stakeholder need
priority: must

A team needs a page to show the state of the Armature issues and the Exciter requirements it names, and the tools it names to know which pages describe them.

### SN-8 Script and integrate the wiki
type: Stakeholder need
priority: should

A developer needs to read and write pages from scripts and assistants with a token that never does more than its owner may, and to hear of changes by webhook.

### SN-9 Take content in and out
type: Stakeholder need
priority: should

A team needs to hand a page to somebody without an account as a document, to bring pages in from Markdown and to move a space between installations.

### SN-10 Rely on the wiki
type: Stakeholder need
priority: must

Everybody needs the wiki to stay fast with a busy team, to be usable with a keyboard and a screen reader, to speak their language, to keep its rules where nobody can get around them and to be restored from a backup.

### SN-11 Have pages checked and read
type: Stakeholder need
priority: should

The owner of a procedure needs it checked by named people before it goes out and needs to know who has read it.
