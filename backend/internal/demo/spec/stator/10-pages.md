# Functional requirements / Pages

### FR-PAG-1 Keep a draft apart from the published page
type: Functional
priority: must
verification: Test
component: Editor
refines: SN-1
issues: STA-13
guide: publishing

While an author edits a published page, Stator shall show readers the page as it was last published until the author
publishes the draft.

### FR-PAG-2 Compare two versions of a page
type: Functional
priority: should
verification: Demonstration
component: History
refines: SN-1
issues: STA-14
guide: history

When a reader picks two versions of a page, Stator shall show what the later one added and removed.
