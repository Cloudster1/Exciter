# Introduction

Stator is the wiki of the suite. People write pages in spaces, keep them in a tree, publish them when they are ready and find them again. They discuss them in comments, decide who may read and write them, and connect them to the issues of Armature, the requirements of Exciter and the runs of Rotor.

This specification says what Stator shall do for the people who write and read its pages and for the tools and scripts that work with them. It holds the needs of its stakeholders, the functional requirements by function area, the quality requirements with criteria that can be measured, the interfaces Stator offers and uses, and the tests that verify each requirement with their latest results.

Each requirement names the issues that built it and the page of the Stator guide that describes it to the people who use it.
