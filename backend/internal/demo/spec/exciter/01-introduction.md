# Introduction

Exciter is the requirements management of the suite. This specification says what Exciter shall do for the people
who write, trace, review and verify requirements, and what it offers the other tools of the suite and uses of them.

It is arranged by function area. Each area has a section of functional requirements, a part of the test specification
and a page in the user guide, which a requirement names in its `guide:` field. The non-functional requirements and the
interfaces follow the areas. Every requirement says its priority, how it is verified and the stakeholder need it
refines, and where Exciter's own development built it, the issues that did.

The areas are projects, requirements, workflow, search, documents, traceability, matrix and coverage, the links to
Armature and to Stator, reviews, baselines, tests, import and export, quality, dashboards, risks and automation.
