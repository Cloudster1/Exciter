# Stakeholder needs

What the people who work with Armature need from it.

### SN-1 See the state of the work at a glance
type: Stakeholder need
priority: must

A team lead needs to see which issues are waiting, in progress and done without opening each of them.

### SN-2 Follow an issue to what it implements
type: Stakeholder need
priority: should

A developer needs to see, on an issue, the requirements and pages other tools of the suite link to it.

### SN-3 Describe work completely and keep its history
type: Stakeholder need
priority: must

A team member needs to write an issue down with everything the team needs to do it, to change many issues without
losing work, and to see afterwards what changed.

### SN-4 Set a project up to fit the way a team works
type: Stakeholder need
priority: must

A project administrator needs to choose the issue types, fields and workflow of a project, and start a new project
from a template instead of from nothing.

### SN-5 Plan sprints and releases that fit the time people have
type: Stakeholder need
priority: must

A scrum master needs to commit to what a team can do, given the days people are away, and to see early when a plan
does not hold.

### SN-6 Find the work and report on it
type: Stakeholder need
priority: must

A team member needs to find any issue by what they remember of it, keep the search, and hand a list to somebody who
works elsewhere.

### SN-7 Be told when the work changes, and let tools react
type: Stakeholder need
priority: should

A team member needs to hear about changes to the work they care about, and an administrator needs other systems to
receive the same events and rules to act on them.

### SN-8 Connect code to the issue it is for
type: Stakeholder need
priority: should

A developer needs the branches, commits, pull requests and test runs of a change to show on its issue, and the issue
to move when the change lands.

### SN-9 Answer customers in time
type: Stakeholder need
priority: should

A support agent needs to receive customers' requests, answer them within agreed times, and keep internal talk away
from the customer.

### SN-10 Let the right people in and keep the others out
type: Stakeholder need
priority: must

An administrator needs people to sign in once for the whole suite, to grant them only the rights they need, and to
see who changed what.

### SN-11 Stay fast and usable
type: Stakeholder need
priority: should

Every user needs pages that answer quickly, that can be read in English or German, and that work on a phone and with
a keyboard.
