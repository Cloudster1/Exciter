# Introduction

Armature is the issue tracker of the suite. This specification says what Armature shall do for the teams that plan
and track their work in it, and what it offers the other tools of the suite and uses of them.

It is arranged by function area. Each area has a section of functional requirements, a part of the test specification
and a page in the user guide, which a requirement names in its `guide:` field. The non-functional requirements and the
interfaces follow the areas. Every requirement says its priority, how it is verified and the stakeholder need it
refines, and where Armature's own development built it, the issues that did.

The areas are issues, projects and workflows, boards, planning, calendars and capacity, search, notifications and
automation, git repositories, the service desk, and access. Terms follow Armature's own: a project holds issues, a
board shows them in columns mapped to statuses, and a role is granted to a person or a group.
